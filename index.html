Arrow keys and the mouse wheel on VFO A tune RX1. Shift makes a ten-times step. Type a frequency and press Enter: 7.147 is MHz, 7147 is kHz, 7147000 or 7.147.000 is hertz. This page does not transmit.

A band button tunes RX1. This browser remembers the last frequency and mode you used on each band.
